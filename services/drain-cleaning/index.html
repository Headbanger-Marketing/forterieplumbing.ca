Questions About the Work

Can I see the camera footage behind a drain finding?

A camera run may show roots, a sag, an offset joint or a cleared blockage, and a clip lets the respondent point to which one the finding concerns.

  • Which drain section the footage covers

    Get the respondent to say first whether the footage covers a single fixture trap, a branch line or the main drain, and where the camera entered. Ask for the clip to be sent or shown, with a note of where along the line each issue sits, and send photos of the affected fixture and the cleanout location if you know it.

  • Compare clearing alone with a line repair

    Compare clearing the blockage alone with a further step such as a repair to the line, using the footage from your Fort Erie home as the deciding evidence. Ask what the clip shows beyond the blockage, and what was removed from the drain.

  • Keep the footage with the written note

    Ask for a copy of the footage or stills to be kept with a written note naming the drain section, what was found and what was removed, such as grease, roots or debris.
