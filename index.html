Plan the request

Asking to see the evidence behind a provider's finding

A provider's finding may rest on drain camera footage, photos taken inside an opening, a reading, a removed part or a label. If you would like to see any of these for your Fort Erie home, say so in the request, and list the symptom, the fixture and where it sits. Describing what you hope to view lets the provider plan what to capture, keep or copy for you before the work begins.

  • Symptom and location

    Note what you saw or heard, which fixture was involved and where it sits, so later photos match it.

  • Evidence you want

    List which records you would like to view, such as video, photos, a reading or a removed part kept for inspection.

  • Earlier records

    Gather any invoices, manuals or past reports on the same item, so the provider can compare them with today's finding.

Before you send

Photos That Help Before the First Call

Photos of the right parts save a lot of back-and-forth. Take these before you send the request and have them ready for the follow-up.

  • Water heater: the label with the model and capacity, the vent connection at the top of the tank, and any rental sticker or agreement.
  • Blocked drain: which fixtures back up, whether it only happens on the lowest floor, and the floor drain or cleanout cap if you can find it.
  • Leak: the stain or damp patch, the water meter dial with every tap closed, and any recent renovation nearby.
  • Sump pump: the open pit and the pump inside it, the discharge pipe where it exits, and any backup pump or alarm.
  • Older supply pipe: the line beside the water meter. Copper is orange-brown, galvanized steel is grey with threaded joints, and lead is dull grey and shows bright silver when scratched lightly with a key.
FAQ

Questions About Your Fort Erie Request

Can I ask to see photos behind a finding at my Fort Erie home?

Yes. State in the request that you would like to view whatever the finding rests on, such as footage, photos, a reading or a removed part. The responding provider must then confirm what can be shown or kept, and put it in the written scope, before any work is agreed. A request alone does not set what will be provided.

If my Fort Erie request lists the records I want to see, has anyone agreed to the work?

No. A request does not establish coverage, availability, price or what evidence exists. Your request is reviewed first by our enquiry team, after which a provider must confirm your address, the work type, their qualifications, written scope, pricing and scheduling before anything is agreed. Until that happens, nothing is booked and you have made no commitment.

Who decides which records are kept or shown for my Fort Erie plumbing problem?

The responding provider decides what can safely be captured, and a removed part may not be kept if it is unsafe to handle or belongs to a rental company. State your wishes in the request, then ask the provider to confirm in writing what will be photographed, recorded or kept before work starts.

Is a single request enough for a drain camera clip and a heater label question?

Yes, if you list each item separately with its own symptom and the evidence you would like to see for it. A camera clip for a slow drain and a rating label for a heater are different records, so a provider can respond to each one without mixing the findings together.

Which photos should I send with a request from my Fort Erie home?

Send clear photos of the fixture or equipment, any rating or model label, the shutoff beside it and the area around the symptom, such as a stain or a pit. Add a short note of when it happens. Do not force a valve that will not turn, and stop using a fixture that backs up.

Will a softener or filter visit show that my water is safe to drink?

No. Servicing a softener or changing a cartridge does not show that water is safe to drink, and a hardness reading is not a safety result. Questions about drinking water belong with the appropriate testing service or a water testing laboratory, and the provider can tell you what evidence they do and do not hold.
